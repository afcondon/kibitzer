The model space

Seed 42, 4 players. Every row is one complete world — an assignment of all 21 cards to hands and envelope — consistent with the public transcript; every column is a card, coloured by where that world puts it. Before E62 the space is too big to show (2.5 × 10^11 legal deals at the start — the dashed stretch of the curve is an estimate anchored at exact endpoints); from E62 on, every consistent world is on the page — all 1,484 of them, the totality, not a sample. Each further event kills the worlds it contradicts. When a column goes monochrome, that card's place is certain — it gets a dot, and an ink ring once the watcher's notebook knows it too. When the three red columns agree, the space itself has solved the murder.
