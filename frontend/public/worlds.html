The model space

Seed 33, 6 players. Every row is one complete world — an assignment of all 21 cards to hands and envelope — consistent with the public transcript; every column is a card, coloured by where that world puts it. Before E66 the space is too big to show (44,460,928,512,000 legal deals at the start, exactly — the dashed stretch of the curve is an estimate anchored at exact endpoints); from E66 on, every consistent world is on the page — all 1,238 of them, the totality, not a sample. Each further event kills the worlds it contradicts. When a column goes monochrome, that card's place is certain — it gets a dot, and an ink ring once the watcher's notebook knows it too. When the three red columns agree, the space itself has solved the murder.
